Instancing, the process of producing an object from a blueprint, has many handy uses. With [[Scenes - Godot|scenes]], it gives you:

- The ability to divide your game into reusable components.
    
- A tool to structure and encapsulate complex systems.
    
- A language to think about your game project's structure in a natural way.
